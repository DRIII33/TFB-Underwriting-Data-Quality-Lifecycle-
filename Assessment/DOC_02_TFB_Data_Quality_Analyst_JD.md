This position is responsible for ensuring the accuracy, completeness, and reliability of our data assets. Candidates 
work closely with cross-functional teams to identify data quality issues, implement solutions, and support data 
governance initiatives with minimal oversight. 
 
MAJOR RESPONSIBIILITIES AND AUTHORITY 
 
Data Quality Assessment and Monitoring: 
Conduct regular data profiling and analysis to assess data quality across various systems. 
Develop and implement data quality metrics and dashboards to monitor ongoing data integrity. 
Identify, document, and communicate data quality issues to relevant stakeholders. 
Data Cleansing and Validation: 
Design and execute data cleansing processes to rectify inaccuracies and inconsistencies. 
Establish and enforce data validation rules to ensure data meets predefined standards. 
Collaborate with IT and business units to implement data quality improvements. 
Root Cause Analysis and Issue Resolution: 
Investigate data anomalies and perform root cause analysis to identify underlying issues. 
Develop and recommend solutions to prevent recurrence of data quality problems. 
Work with data owners to implement corrective actions and process enhancements. 
Data Governance and Documentation: 
Support data governance initiatives by maintaining data dictionaries and metadata repositories. 
Document data quality processes, standards, and best practices. 
Participate in the development and enforcement of data governance policies. 
Other duties as assigned. 
 
MINIMUM POSITION QUALIFICATIONS 
 
At least one of the following: 
●​
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Management, or a related field, and 
foundational experience in data quality analysis or a similar role. 
●​
Associate degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Management, or a related field, and 
proven experience in data quality analysis or a similar role. 
Technical Skills: 
Proficiency in SQL for data querying and analysis. 
Experience with data quality tools (e.g., Informatica Data Quality, Talend, Data Ladder). 
Familiarity with data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI) is a plus. 

 
Analytical and Communication Skills: 
Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret complex data sets. 
Excellent problem-solving abilities and attention to detail. 
Effective communication skills to convey data quality issues and solutions to non-technical stakeholders. 
Preferred Qualifications: 
Experience with data governance frameworks and practices. 
Knowledge of data management principles and data lifecycle management. 
Certifications in data quality or data management (e.g., IQCP, CDMP) are advantageous.
